How Select 5 lets gold, silver and offshore compete

Most mutual fund portfolios hold Indian equity funds and treat gold or overseas funds as an afterthought. Select 5 makes them compete for a place on equal terms.

3 min readPickMyFund

The usual way

A typical portfolio picks funds by their past three- or five-year returns, holds mostly Indian equity, and adds a fixed slice of gold or an overseas fund, if at all. That slice rarely changes, whether gold is in a strong phase or a weak one.

Five funds, three kinds of slot

Select 5 holds five funds, each reset to 20% at every quarterly review.

  • Two strength slots (40%). Sector funds, gold funds and silver funds are ranked together by their average six-month return. The top two groups win a place. In some quarters that means defence and healthcare; in others it has meant gold and silver.
  • One overseas slot (20%). Overseas markets such as US technology, the broad US market, Taiwan, Japan, Europe and emerging markets are ranked the same way. The slot takes the best fund in the leading market that is open for new money. If no overseas option beats a simple arbitrage fund, the slot stays in arbitrage.
  • Two core slots (40%). Two steady diversified funds, chosen across large, flexi, mid and small cap for return relative to volatility, give the portfolio its anchor.

Why let them compete

Gold, silver and overseas markets often move differently from Indian shares. A fixed allocation ignores that. Letting them compete means they earn a bigger role when they are strong and step aside when they are not, while the core slots keep the portfolio grounded.

Guard rails

  • Only funds above ₹500 crore with at least a one-year record are considered, and only one fund per fund house.
  • A fund already held stays while it remains in the top three of its group, so the portfolio does not churn on small changes.
  • Signals are checked every month, but funds normally change only at the quarterly review in January, April, July and October.
  • New money waits in an arbitrage fund and moves in over three months, rather than all at once.

What to keep in mind

Sector, gold, silver and overseas funds can be volatile, and a six-month trend can reverse. Overseas funds also carry currency risk and can close to new money when industry limits are reached. Select 5 is a model, not a promise of returns, and every investment goes through a suitability check against your risk profile first.
